## 123Pack - a list-making app for iOS<br>
## The app let you organise the things you need for your holiday<br>

Student: Oliver Hartmann

123Pack is a digital packing list application designed to help travellers organize and manage their packing requirements. Users can create individual trips, define travel dates and add items from a personal item library to their packing lists. Items can be organized into categories, assigned quantities and marked as packed. This allows users to keep track of their packing progress and ensures a structured overview of all required travel essentials. By combining trip management with customizable packing lists, 123Pack aims to simplify travel preparation and reduce the risk of forgetting important items.

The app has been developed using the folling coding languages and frameworks:

- Swift
- SwiftUI
- SwiftData
- Foundation
